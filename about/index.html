About

Electrical engineering, pointed at learning and control.

I'm a B.Tech student at IIT (BHU) Varanasi and a junior core member of the IIT BHU Robotics Club. I'm looking for research internships in multi-agent learning, RL for control and robotics.

1Approach

I came to robotics through electrical engineering, which shapes how I work: I want to know why something works before I trust it. That's why I wrote a neural network in NumPy before PyTorch, and why I'm working through Ogata rather than treating control as a black box behind a library call.

The direction is the overlap of learning and control. Reinforcement learning can find behaviours nobody hand-designed; control theory says when a system stays stable. Real robots need both, and the interesting problems sit in the tension between them.Multi-agent systems are the stated direction. My work so far is single-agent and constrained RL, plus the robotics infrastructure to test it.

I learn by building something and letting it fail. A servo that freezes mid-gesture teaches you about blocking loops faster than any chapter on real-time systems, and an arm that reaches perfectly but cannot insert a peg teaches you what a reward function leaves out.
